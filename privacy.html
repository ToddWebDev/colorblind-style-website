Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Colorblind Style is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Camera Usage

The app uses your device camera solely to identify colors of clothing items. All image processing happens entirely on your device. No images or camera data are ever transmitted to our servers or any third party.

Data Storage

Any data you create within the app — such as saved color matches — is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device.

Third Party Services

Colorblind Style does not use any third party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. In-app purchases are processed by Apple and are subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at webdevtodd@gmail.com.
